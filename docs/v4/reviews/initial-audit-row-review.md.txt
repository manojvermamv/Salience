**Fix:** Give each row structured historical/local evidence entries with the qualified commit, artifact digest or an explicit unavailable reason, environment reference/scope, immutable evidence reference/hash, and a precise freshness reason. ### I3 — Current-entry dependencies and later-phase obligations remain mixed

**Location:** `docs/v4/p1-rg0-rg1-initial-gap-audit.json:220`, `:345`, `:407`, `:515`, `:537`; final-entry rule at `docs/v4/p1-rg0-rg1-initial-gap-audit.md:107`.

**Fix:** Split each affected row into current P1/RG0/RG1 entry residuals, preservation/regression obligations for the new increments, and explicitly deferred phase-owned work. If the authoritative blueprint's full-production statements leave that boundary unresolved, record an explicit phase-ownership decision/blocker; do not silently waive the stronger requirement or make P2 entry depend on P2 implementation.
